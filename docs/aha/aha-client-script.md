# AHA — what to say to a client

Keep it to the problem it solves: nobody has to call us at 2am to get into a truck.

## The 10-second version — say this one
> "We have an assistant called AHA. If your driver's at the lot at 4am and can't get in, they text AHA and get the gate code and the lockbox code right there. No waiting on us."

## When you hand off the job — the natural moment
> "One more thing — on your job page there's a 5-digit code. Give it to your driver. If they ever need the gate or lockbox code after hours, they text that number on the page and AHA gives it to them. And if you're texting us from the number we have on file, you don't even need the code."

## If they ask
- **"Is it a person?"** → "No, it's automated, and it tells you that up front. Anything it can't handle it hands to our on-call team, or you call the 24/7 line."
- **"Is that safe?"** → "It verifies before it gives out anything. Job code plus the VIN or the driver's name. Every release is logged and we get notified."
- **"What else does it do?"** → "Your booking — what's out, what's back, dates, delivery address, who your agent is. And you can leave a message for your agent any hour and they'll follow up."
- **"What if it's an emergency?"** → "Tell it that and it texts our on-call team your name and number. For anything immediate, call us — that's still fastest."
- **"What can't it do?"** → "It won't quote you pricing or availability. That's us."

## Paste this in a text or email
> Heads up — after hours your driver can text AHA, our automated assistant, at (747) 335-1665 for the gate code and lockbox code. They'll need the 5-digit code on your job page plus the VIN last 4 or the driver's name. Anything urgent, call us at (888) 477-7335.

## Don't say
- Don't call it AI or a chatbot — call it our after-hours assistant
- Don't promise it does pricing, availability, or booking changes
- Don't say "it'll call you back" — it files the message, the agent follows up
