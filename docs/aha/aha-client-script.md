# AHA — what to say to a client

Keep it to the problem it solves: nobody has to call us at 2am to get into a truck.

## The 10-second version — say this one
> "We have an assistant called AHA. If your driver's at the lot at 4am and can't get in, they text AHA from their own cell, give their name, and get the gate code and the lockbox code right there."

## When you hand off the job — the natural moment
> "Make sure we've got your driver's cell number on the job. That's all AHA needs — they text it, say their name, and they're verified. They don't even have to be standing at the truck, so if they've already parked and gone home they can still get a code."

## If they ask
- **"Is it a person?"** → "No, it's automated, and it tells you that up front. If it can't help, it gets our on-call team."
- **"What if we didn't give you a number?"** → "Then they use the 5-digit code on your job page plus the VIN last 4 or the driver's name. Easier to just get us the number."
- **"Is that safe?"** → "It only works from a number already on that job, and it only opens that job's truck. Every release is logged and we get notified."
- **"What else does it do?"** → "Your booking — what's out, what's back, dates, delivery address, who your agent is. And you can leave a message for your agent any hour."
- **"What if it's an emergency?"** → "Tell it that. It texts our on-call team and gives you their direct numbers so you can call a person right then."
- **"What can't it do?"** → "It won't quote you pricing or availability. That's us."

## Paste this in a text or email
> Heads up — after hours your driver can text AHA, our automated assistant, at (747) 335-1665 for the gate code and lockbox code. As long as we have their cell number on the job, they just give their name. Make sure we've got that number.

## Don't say
- **Don't call (888) 477-7335 a 24/7 line.** It's business hours. AHA is the after-hours front door
- Don't call AHA an AI or a chatbot — call it our after-hours assistant
- Don't promise it does pricing, availability, or booking changes
- Don't say "it'll call you back" — it files the message, the agent follows up
