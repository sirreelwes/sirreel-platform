Meet AHA — say it AH-ha

SirReel's automated After Hours Assistant. It answers texts at (747) 335-1665, any hour of the night.

1Text it a fleet question

  • “Who's on Cube 27?” → the job, the dates, the driver's name and cell, the delivery address
  • “Has Lunch Rush come back?” → still out, or every unit checked in
  • “What's on SR-JOB-0231?” → units, dates, contacts and their numbers
  • Works from your phone, no login. Your mobile has to be on your HQ user record first — set it on /admin/assistant under Mobile

2It takes the 2am calls for you

  • A driver locked out at the gate texts AHA instead of calling you
  • AHA checks them against the job, then gives the gate code and that truck's lockbox code
  • It only opens a truck on a live job, and only for a number or name already on that job
  • Every release is logged and emailed to the team, with who got it

3Three things fleet has to keep current

  • Lockbox code on every truck. Fleet page, vehicle summary panel. No code on file means AHA can't release it — the driver is stuck and the team gets an email saying so
  • The gate code. /admin/assistant. If it's wrong, nobody gets in
  • Driver cell numbers on the job. That's how AHA knows a driver is really theirs

4When it can't help

  • Driver at one of our trucks that AHA can't verify → it texts on-call their name and number, and releases nothing
  • A real emergency → it texts on-call and gives the caller their direct numbers
  • The office line (888) 477-7335 is business hours only. AHA is the after-hours front door

5It will never

  • Give out a code for a job that's wrapped, cancelled, or not running today
  • Quote pricing or availability
  • Pretend to be a person — it says it's automated every time
