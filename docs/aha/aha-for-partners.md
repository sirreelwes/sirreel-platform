# AHA for partners — one page

## What it is
- SirReel's After Hours Assistant — automated, on 24/7, texting from (747) 335-1665
- For a partner (King Kong, PowerTrip Rentals) it is the after-hours contact for your drivers and delivery crews on SirReel jobs

## What your drivers and delivery contacts get
- Day-of texts from SirReel about the booking: confirmation, a moved call time, the delivery address, a gate-access change, the return window
- Reply to any of those texts, any hour, and AHA answers — no waiting on office hours
- AHA recognises the number on your booking (the driver or delivery contact you named) and greets them by first name
- It knows which unit and which SirReel job that number is on, so nobody has to explain the booking from scratch

## What AHA can do for them
- Directions to the SirReel lot (8500 Lankershim Blvd, Sun Valley), hours, and the 24/7 line (888) 477-7335
- Stuck at a SirReel vehicle after hours and unable to verify → AHA texts the on-call team their name and callback number so a person steps in
- A genuine emergency — a safety issue, or a blocked on-the-clock production → the on-call team is texted their name, number and what is wrong
- Lot gate access, when a SirReel job needs it: the production's job code plus the VIN last 4 or the driver's name, or the production contact can text on the driver's behalf
- Gear setup walkthroughs for units on the job, with a link to the printable guide

## How your people opt in to texts
- The consent checkbox beside the mobile number on your booking page (driver, or delivery contact for delivered equipment) or on the driver's own profile
- Or text START to (747) 335-1665
- STOP opts out immediately; HELP returns SirReel's email and phone
- No marketing, ever — booking notifications and replies only
- Automated texts hold during quiet hours, 9pm–6am Pacific

## Want more for your dispatch?
- SirReel can add a partner dispatcher's number by hand, scoped to one job, so they can ask AHA for that job's units, dates, delivery address and SirReel agent, and leave a message for the agent
- Ask your SirReel contact — access is granted and revoked by SirReel, and every grant is audited

## What AHA will not do
- Quote rates, pricing or availability — for you or for the production
- Book, change or cancel a sub-rental, or edit your partner page
- Release a SirReel gate or lockbox code without verification
- Share client or production contact numbers with a driver
- Pretend to be a SirReel employee, or speak for your company
